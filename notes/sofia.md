# Interview: Sofia, student house of five

> **Fictional.** Written for the PRD Assistant demo. Sofia and everyone she mentions are made up.

- **Participant:** Sofia, 23, master's student
- **Household:** five students sharing a house, each with their own room and one shared kitchen
- **Shopping:** everyone buys their own food; shared items get bought by whoever notices first
- **Lists today:** a house group chat, and a shared spreadsheet for costs

**Interviewer:** What do you buy together, and what do you buy alone?

**Sofia:** Food is mostly our own. The shared things are toilet paper, dish soap, oil, salt, bin bags, that kind of stuff. They run out at the worst moment, and then three people buy toilet paper on the same day.

**Interviewer:** How do you decide who buys what?

**Sofia:** Someone posts "we're out of dish soap" in the house chat, and then it's either a race or nobody does it. What I'd love is a way to say "I'm getting this", so the others know it's taken. Just claiming an item would stop the double buying.

**Interviewer:** And paying for it?

**Sofia:** We keep a spreadsheet of who paid for shared stuff and settle up once a month. Honestly, it's the most annoying part of living together. If the list remembered who bought what, it would save a lot of arguing. But maybe that's a different app.

**Interviewer:** Would you put your own food on the shared list?

**Sofia:** No. My own shopping is my business. I'd want my personal items somewhere the house can't see, or at least clearly apart from the shared things.

**Interviewer:** What did you think of list apps you've used?

**Sofia:** We tried one last year. Five people adding stuff all day, and it sent a notification for every single item. If an app notifies me every time someone adds something, I uninstall it. That's literally why we stopped using that one: two of us deleted it in the first week.

**Interviewer:** How would you want to be told, then?

**Sofia:** I'd open it when I'm going to the shop anyway. Maybe I'd want to know when someone else bought something I'd claimed, but not about every bottle of ketchup.

**Interviewer:** Is anyone in the house hard to get on board?

**Sofia:** Jonas refuses to install new apps. He'd use it if it opened in the browser from a link, without making an account. Otherwise he'll keep posting in the chat, and we'll have two lists again.

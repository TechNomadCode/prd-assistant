# Interview: Maya, couple in a city flat

> **Fictional.** Written for the PRD Assistant demo. Maya and everyone she mentions are made up.

- **Participant:** Maya, 34, designer, works full time
- **Household:** Maya and her partner Tom, in a two-bedroom flat
- **Shopping:** small shops on the way home from work, three or four times a week
- **Lists today:** messages to each other in a chat app

**Interviewer:** How do you and Tom keep track of what you need?

**Maya:** We message each other. Tom sends "we're out of rice" at ten in the morning, I send "eggs?" at lunch, and by six it's buried under photos and weekend plans. When I'm standing in the shop, I scroll back through the whole day trying to rebuild the list.

**Interviewer:** What goes wrong with that?

**Maya:** We buy things twice. Last month we ended up with three bags of rice, because we both saw the message and both stopped at a shop. The opposite happens too: I think Tom is getting the bread, he thinks I am, and nobody gets it.

**Interviewer:** If you had a shared list, how would you want it to work?

**Maya:** The moment one of us puts something on it, the other should know. Honestly, I want a notification for every item someone adds. I pass two supermarkets on my way home, and if Tom adds lemons at five past five, I want my phone to tell me right then, not when I happen to open the app at home.

**Interviewer:** And while you're in the shop?

**Maya:** When I tick something off, it has to disappear from Tom's list straight away, so he doesn't buy it too. That's the whole point. If the list on his phone is even ten minutes behind mine, we're back to three bags of rice.

**Interviewer:** How detailed are the items you add?

**Maya:** Very. "Yoghurt" isn't enough, because Tom will come home with the sweet one with the cartoon cow on it. I want to write "plain Greek yoghurt, the big tub" and have that be one item, not a paragraph in a chat.

**Interviewer:** Have you tried a list app before?

**Maya:** One, for about a month. It was fine, but setting it up meant both of us making accounts and confirming emails, and Tom gave up halfway. If I can't add him to the list in under a minute, it won't happen.

**Interviewer:** What would tell you it's working?

**Maya:** A month without buying anything twice. And not scrolling back through our chat in the middle of the shop.

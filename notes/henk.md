# Interview: Henk, retired couple

> **Fictional.** Written for the PRD Assistant demo. Henk and everyone he mentions are made up.

- **Participant:** Henk, 68, retired
- **Household:** Henk and his wife Ria; their daughter Ineke lives ten minutes away and helps with the shopping
- **Shopping:** Henk walks to the supermarket most mornings
- **Lists today:** Ineke texts him, or Ria writes on the back of an envelope

**Interviewer:** How does Ineke help with the shopping?

**Henk:** Ria doesn't walk well anymore, so Ineke keeps an eye on what we need. She comes by on Sundays, looks in the cupboards and texts me what's missing during the week. Sometimes she phones while I'm already in the shop, which is a bit late.

**Interviewer:** Have you used a list app before?

**Henk:** Ineke put one on my phone last year. She set it all up; I didn't have to do anything. But the buttons were tiny and the text was light grey on white. I couldn't read it in the shop without my other glasses, so I went back to the envelope.

**Interviewer:** What would make an app usable for you?

**Henk:** Big letters. Black on white or white on black, I don't mind, as long as it's clear. Few buttons. I want to see the list and tick things off, that's all. I don't need menus.

**Interviewer:** How would you want to know when Ineke adds something?

**Henk:** I'd like my phone to ping me every time she puts something on the list. Every single item, yes. Otherwise I won't think to look. I'm not someone who opens apps just to check. If it beeps, I look, and then I know to buy the coffee.

**Interviewer:** Does anything about the items themselves matter?

**Henk:** The right brand. Ria drinks one kind of coffee and one kind only. If Ineke writes "coffee", I come home with the wrong one. It would help if she could add a photo of the packet, or if the app remembered what we usually buy.

**Interviewer:** Who else should see the list?

**Henk:** Ineke and me. Maybe our son when he visits, but he lives abroad. Ria would rather tell me than use the phone.

**Interviewer:** What would you count as a success?

**Henk:** That Ineke stops having to phone me in the shop. And that I come home with the right coffee.

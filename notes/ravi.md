# Interview: Ravi, couple who plans meals

> **Fictional.** Written for the PRD Assistant demo. Ravi and everyone he mentions are made up.

- **Participant:** Ravi, 37, accountant
- **Household:** Ravi and his husband Joost
- **Shopping:** one big shop on Sunday after planning the week's meals, and one top-up midweek
- **Lists today:** a meal plan on a whiteboard, copied by hand into a list

**Interviewer:** Walk me through your Sunday.

**Ravi:** We sit down with coffee and decide the week's dinners. Joost picks three, I pick three, and Friday is leftovers. Then I go through the recipes, write every ingredient on a list, check the cupboards and cross off what we already have. It takes us almost an hour, and most of that is copying.

**Interviewer:** What goes wrong?

**Ravi:** Quantities. If two recipes both need an onion, the list says "onion" once, and we're one short on Thursday. Or it says "chickpeas" and I buy one can when we needed three. I'd want the list to add those up: two recipes, two onions.

**Interviewer:** How do you organise the list?

**Ravi:** By section of the shop, otherwise it's chaos: fruit and veg, then the fridge, then dry goods, then household. I rewrite it in that order every week, which is silly.

**Interviewer:** What's on the list every week?

**Ravi:** Coffee, oat milk, eggs, bread, bananas, and washing-up liquid every other week. I'd like to mark those as regulars and have them show up without typing them.

**Interviewer:** How do you and Joost share the work?

**Ravi:** I plan and Joost shops, because his office is next to the supermarket. So he needs exactly my list on his phone. If I think of something on Tuesday, I want to add it and know it's there when he goes on Wednesday.

**Interviewer:** Does food get wasted?

**Ravi:** More than I'd like. Half a bag of spinach every week, because I buy it for a recipe we end up skipping. If the list knew which items belong to which meal, I could drop a meal and its ingredients would go with it.

**Interviewer:** How would you know a list app was worth it?

**Ravi:** If Sunday planning took twenty minutes instead of an hour. And if we stopped throwing out spinach.
